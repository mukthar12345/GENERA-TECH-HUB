Our Story

Genera Tech Hub was founded in 2026 with a simple yet powerful vision: to make quality technology accessible, affordable, and reliable for everyone in Nigeria.

What started as a passion for technology has grown into a trusted tech hub serving customers across Nigeria. From day one, we've built our reputation on transparency, quality, and exceptional customer service.

Today, Genera Tech Hub is the go-to destination for buying, selling, repairing, and swapping phones, laptops, and accessories. We're proud to be a part of Nigeria's growing tech ecosystem.

  • Quality products — 100% genuine devices
  • Unique prices — best value in the market
  • Expert repairs with warranty
