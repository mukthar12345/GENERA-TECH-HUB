🔒 Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information at Genera Tech Hub.

Last Updated: January 2026

👋 Welcome

Welcome to Genera Tech Hub. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

📋 Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number
  • Device Information: Brand, model, issue descriptions (for repairs)
  • Transaction Data: Products you purchase, repairs you request, trade-ins
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through our contact forms and WhatsApp
  • Account Data: Login credentials when you create an account

🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for:

  • Processing your repair bookings and product orders
  • Communicating with you via WhatsApp about your requests
  • Managing your account and providing customer support
  • Improving our services and website experience
  • Sending updates about your repairs or orders
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring security

💬 WhatsApp Communication

Many features on our website redirect you to WhatsApp for direct communication. When you contact us via WhatsApp:

  • Your messages go directly to our official business WhatsApp (08081302228)
  • We do NOT store your WhatsApp conversation history on our servers
  • WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy applies to your conversation data
  • We may reference these conversations to process your requests

🔐 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL encryption for all website traffic
  • Secure database storage via Supabase
  • Password encryption for user accounts
  • Limited access to customer data (CEO and authorized staff only)
  • Regular security audits

🍪 Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in between sessions
  • Remember your preferences
  • Track website visits for analytics (anonymous data)
  • Improve our website experience

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect website functionality.

🤝 Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Supabase: For secure data storage and authentication
  • WhatsApp/Meta: For customer communication
  • Netlify: For website hosting

These services have their own privacy policies that govern their use of your data.

👤 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • File a complaint about our data practices

👶 Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
